⚠
Food allergy? Tell us before you order.

Add allergy details to your inquiry or call us before booking. We'll talk through the menu before we cook.

We don't intentionally use nuts in our recipes, but some ingredients may be made in facilities that handle peanuts, tree nuts, soy, eggs, wheat, fish, shellfish, and dairy.

📅
Book 14+ days ahead

To guarantee your preferred date, time, and pricing, catering orders should be placed 14 days or more in advance of the requested pickup or delivery date. Orders placed with fewer than 14 days' notice are subject to availability and may be subject to an additional surcharge.

📋
Pricing notes

Pricing and availability are subject to change. Taxes, delivery fees, and gratuity are not included in the prices listed above.
