Sample Catering Menu

What we cook

Build your menu from our real menu. Pricing scales with headcount — the quote includes per-person totals.

Prime Brisket House signature

Smoked low and slow. Available by the half-pound on the truck; bulk-priced for catering.

Pulled Pork Crowd favorite

Pork shoulder smoked overnight on real wood — crusted bark on the outside, juicy and tender inside, hand-pulled. Brioche-bun option or by the pound.

Pulled Chicken Easy crowd-pleaser

Whole chickens dry-rubbed and smoked over real wood until they pull clean off the bone. Brioche-bun option or by the pound.

St. Louis Ribs Half or full rack

Hand-rubbed, slow-smoked, sauce on the side.

Sweet & Sticky Pork Belly Melts in your mouth

Slow-smoked pork belly bites in signature BBQ sauce.

Smoke-Fried Chicken Wings BBQ or Habanero Jerk

House rub, smoked then deep-fried. Habanero Jerk is extremely spicy — ask first.

Smash Burger Bar Classic, BBQ, Meat-Ur-Match

Three burger options for events. The Meat-Ur-Match is brisket + pulled pork stacked — a beast.

Smoked Mac, Beans, Salads Sides

Smoked mac & cheese, baked beans, cucumber salad, potato salad, tater tots.
